# Architecture

Purpose: how the code is split (logic vs. drawing) and where each system lives.

## Files

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `lib/game.dart` | ALL game logic, pure Dart, no Flutter: `Stats`, `Unit`, `Shot`, `Game`, enums `Item` (each with its `ItemSlot` kind and grade), `Relic`, `Side`, `Sfx`. |
| `lib/hero_moves.dart` | `part of` game.dart: the Hero skills' forced movement (knockback, leap, drag, charge, brace; `Move`, `Carry` (with its `CarryLook` for the drawing, #88), `Charge`, `Brace`, the `HeroMoves` extension on `Game`; units-and-combat.md), and the seven movement Hero skills (their rules, targets and casts: `_wantsMove`, `_castMove`, the `<id>K` numbers), which hero_skills.dart sends here. game.dart calls it: `_moves` in `tick`, `braced` in `_moveToward`, `_firm` / `_passesThrough` in separation and `_makeWay`. |
| `lib/battle_focus.dart` | `part of` game.dart, pure Dart: the camera's battle point (#85; camera-and-field.md): its consts (`contactSlack`, `reachPull`, `fightGap`, `minFight`, `switchRatio`, `switchHold`, `contactGrace`, `frontTrim`, `frontGap`, `approachLead`, `frontIn`, `focusSlack`, `focusSpeed`, `camEase`, `spawnClear`, `spawnLead`, `keepUpInset`, `tierWeight`), the owner's switches (`approachFollowsFront`, `fightPick`), `MeetMode`, and `extension BattleFocus on Game` (`contactPoints`, `fightsOf`, `fights`, `troopFront`, `aim`, `viewGoal`, `spawnX`, `keepUpShift`, and `_aimView`, which `tick` calls where the cam eased before). The state is on `Game` (`viewMid`, `meetX`, `fightX`, `meetMode`) and `Unit.joining`. |
| `lib/hero_skills.dart` | `part of` game.dart, pure Dart: the Heroes' own skills (#48): `HeroSkill` (all 45, `heroSkillPool`), the effect store (`Status`, `Effect`, `Unit.effects`) and `extension HeroSkills on Game` with the rules, the effects and the hook bodies game.dart calls (units-and-combat.md, "Hero skills"). A new Hero skill plugs in here only. |
| `lib/monster_skills.dart` | `part of` game.dart, pure Dart: the monsters' own skills (#59): `MonsterSkill` (a type's signature, cast from high elite up: `monsterSkillOf`), the wind-up and its cancel, the cap on a skill's blow (`monsterSkillCap`, `monsterSkillCapOf`), the `<id>K` numbers and `extension MonsterSkills on Game` (`_monsterSkillTurn` in `tick`, `wantsMonsterSkill`, `_castMonsterSkill`, `windTargetsOf` for the tell; units-and-combat.md, "Monster skills"). A new monster skill plugs in here. |
| `lib/monster_skill_moves.dart` | `part of` game.dart, pure Dart: #59 branch 2a, the four monster skills that ride the forced movement and the looks (Sticky Glob, Swoop, Pounce, Trample): their rules, casts and `<id>K` numbers (`extension MonsterSkillMoves on Game`: `_wantsMoveSkill`, `_castMoveSkill`), sent here from one arm in each of monster_skills.dart's switches (units-and-combat.md, "Monster skills"). |
| `lib/monster_skill_signatures.dart` | `part of` game.dart, pure Dart: #59 branch 2b, the rest of the high elites' signature skills, handed over by one arm in each of monster_skills.dart's switches (`extension MonsterSkillSignatures on Game`): Maul (`maulK`), Curse of Frailty (`curseOfFrailtyK`), Reassemble (`reassembleK`, `_reassembles` from `_hurt`) and the holy blow (`_holyHurt`, `Game._holy`); units-and-combat.md, "Branch 2b". |
| `lib/boss_skills.dart` | `part of` game.dart, pure Dart: the mini bosses' and the boss's skills (#59 branch 3): the mini-boss skills and the boss's moments (`MonsterRank`), their `<id>K` numbers, the gap between a mini boss's two (`bossSkillGap`), the moment's rule (`bossMomentK`), the monsters a skill brings in (`_bringIn`: raised minions) and Grave Call's graves; `extension BossSkills on Game` (`_wantsBossSkill`, `_castBossSkill`, `_monsterFell`; units-and-combat.md, "Mini bosses and the boss"). |
| `lib/awaken_skills.dart` | `part of` game.dart, pure Dart: the troops' awakening skills (#88): `AwakenSkill` (one per troop type, per level: cooldown, `costAt`, `titleAt`; `built` marks those with rules), `awakenSkillOf`, the `<id>K` numbers, the book's lines (`awakenLines`, `awakenSkillChange`) and `extension AwakenSkills on Game`: the clock and its stagger, the MP rule (`_savingForAwaken`, `mpWanted`), the rules and the casts (units-and-combat.md "Awakening skills"). |

## The logic/drawing contract

- `Game.tick(dt)` advances everything: spawns, movement, attacks, shots, deaths, loot, win, and the camera (`cam`).
  It returns early once `over`, and while `paused`.
- `Game` is told the view width: `Board.update` sets `g.viewW = size.x / _scale` before each tick.
- `Game` never draws. It queues effects for the UI, which drains them every frame:
  - `sfx` (sounds);
  - `impacts` (`(pos, name)` bursts: `hit_<attacker>`, `heal`, `holy` (a troop healer's smite on a dark
    monster), `upgrade`, `summon`, `summon_<tier>` for a raised minion above normal, `fireball` and
    `shards` for a wizard's plain shot and Arcane Blast landing (`shotHitFx`); a troop of an awakened type adds `_awake<level>` to its own, `awakeFx`, except on
    `holy`);
  - `casts` (troops that just cast their skill: its name pops up over them), `heroCasts` (a Hero's own, in
    gold), `monsterCasts` (a monster beginning its skill's wind-up, in red; #59) and `awakenCasts` (a troop's
    awakening skill with its level, cast or just unlocked; #88).
- The UI draws the skills' `lineShots`, `zones`, `beams` and `rings` (hero_shots.dart) as they are; the game
  drops each when it is done (a beam or a ring when its time is up or its unit falls). The troops' skills leave
  their looks there too (#81), so each look is in the game where the tests reach it.
- The UI reads state and draws it. Any number, rule or collision belongs in `game.dart`, where the
  tests can reach it.

## Coordinates

- World units: 1 tile = 16. The field is `rows`(8) × 16 = `fieldH` 128 tall.
- The parallax band above the field is `backdropH` 64.
- x grows right, and y is measured from the field's top.
- `Board._scale` maps world to screen; `_toWorld(screen)` goes back.
- Sprites are Tiny RPG 100×100 frames, feet on row 59 (`_feet`, where a unit's ground shadow sits), drawn at 1 sprite
  pixel = 1 world unit. The Town, which draws no shadow, puts each type's feet's last row (`townFeet`, row 56) on its
  line instead (lib/town_life.dart, decisions-log 292).

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `units` | Troops and monsters in one list. `troops` / `enemies` are views of it. |
| `baseX` | The back line: zone start and life-loss line. |
| `capacity` | How many troops may be alive at once (bought 2 at a time); `zoneCols`, the deploy width, follows from it. |
